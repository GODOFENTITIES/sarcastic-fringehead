Recommended reading order
If you want to build a rigorous foundation without reading everything at once, I recommend this order:
1. Constitutional AI — understand principle-guided model behaviour.
2. GuardBench — learn how to evaluate guardrails quantitatively.
3. CAPTURE — understand the false-positive/false-negative trade-off.
4. Black-Box Access is Insufficient for Rigorous AI Audits — understand audit access.
5. AI Governance: A Systematic Literature Review — map the broader research field.
6. Exploring Laws of Robotics — explore the connection between AI constitutions and institutional rule-making.
7. Meaningful Contestability — understand appeals and challenges to automated decisions.
